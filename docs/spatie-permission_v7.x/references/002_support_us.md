# Support Spatie

> Source: https://spatie.be/docs/laravel-permission/v7/support-us

Spatie dedicates significant time and effort to building and maintaining open source packages. You can support their work by purchasing their premium products at [spatie.be/open-source](https://spatie.be/open-source).

They also welcome postcards from users — let them know which of their packages you are using in your projects.
